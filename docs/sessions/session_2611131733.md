## Summary of actions

Earlier the same day PR #18 was merged and PR #19 (the Class Lists page) was pushed with its gates green. Daniel then tried the page with his Foundational export and found the address columns of the workbook empty. He asked for the address to come from the form question "Enter your complete Postal Address, including country and postal code", with two rules: state at most 3 letters, country in capitals.

Cause: Corsizio's exports now carry its own address columns (`Address`, `Address: City`, `Address: State/Province`, `Address: Country`, `Address: Zip/Postal Code`), empty in all 9 rows, before the form questions. `parse` took the first column containing "postal", which is now the zip column. Fixed by looking for "postal address". Full Australian state names now become their abbreviation, and "Western Australia" or "South Australia" at the end is the state, not the country.
